# No jump from the floor reaches Route 99 act 1's downward springs.

About: Springs; Route 99 act 1; A character jumping

Proof: #480; springs-up

At the spring at 5968,1632, a full held jump from the floor under it never brings the spring onto the screen, and a character 30 pixels to its left is inside the pillar. Under those at 6928,1872, 976,1104, 3344,192 and 7840,1968, Sonic let go from just below the spring falls for a long way, lands on a slope or a curve and runs off, so there's no ground under them to jump from. Put under a spring on his way up, as a start point can, he's thrown down with every frame the same as the original's, because the original does the same with the same values, which doesn't show a player can get there. So no recording shows a downward throw yet, and the way a player reaches one has still to be found.
