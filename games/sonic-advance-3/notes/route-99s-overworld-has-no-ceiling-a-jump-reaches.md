# On Route 99's overworld, no jump reaches a ceiling until a button's spring can throw a character up.

About: A character jumping; Route 99's overworld; character-jumping-walls

Proof: #477; character-jumping-walls

Near where Sonic arrives the walkway has open sky over it, and from the floor of the shaft under the breakable wall, a held jump's highest point stays a few pixels short of the overhang. What lies further on needs the red button that first shows at frame 893 of a recording that falls down the shaft, and the spring it brings up. So character-jumping-walls stops at frame 889, before the button, and ceilings have a piece of their own. Sprites' tile numbers differ by a few from the original's there, as the objects not made yet hold tiles in the original, which doesn't change the picture.
