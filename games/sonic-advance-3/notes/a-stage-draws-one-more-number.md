# A stage draws one more of the game's numbers as it starts than the partners' two.

About: The team partner; A character running; character-running

Proof: #476; character-running

Each character's start draws one number for the partner's choices, so two in all, yet with only those two the partner chose differently from the original under most starting numbers, usually in its first press after the leader moved away. With one more drawn at the stage's start, seeds 5 and 7 match on all 1,100 frames of character-running, and with two more seed 7 doesn't. Writing the original's number later, as late as the last frame before the stage, doesn't take the need away. Which function draws it wasn't found. Other starting numbers differed where the partner looks up or down or jumps, which weren't made then, and they haven't been tried since.
