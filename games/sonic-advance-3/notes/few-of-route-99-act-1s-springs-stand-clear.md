# Few of Route 99 act 1's springs stand clear of objects not made yet.

About: Springs; Route 99 act 1; springs-up; springs-up-rolling; springs-right; springs-left

Proof: #480; springs-up; springs-up-rolling; springs-right; springs-left

What stands near most springs, such as an enemy or a rail, differs from the first frame or soon after. At the spring at 6272,2064, a character standing a few pixels left of it has the objects further left on the screen unless the camera has moved on: walking in from x 6258 shows some of them at frames 15 to 23, and from x 6264 none, though there he touches the spring on his first frame, as he does at x 6284 on its other side. A spin dash from x 6258 moves the camera far enough first. The spring at 6480,1440 can be walked into with nothing in the way, and its recording matches until Sonic reaches a rail on his way down. A character's speed as the spring touches it decides how the throw looks, so a roll into a spring gets the fast one. The act's only diagonal spring, a small one at 9128,1488, differs from the first frame by what stands around it, so no recording shows a diagonal throw yet.
