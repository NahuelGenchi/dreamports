# Tails, thrown by the leader on an object, stands up sooner than its pose asks, and the reason wasn't found.

About: sub_800E880; sub_800CCB0; partner-tag-action; partner-tag-action-moves

Proof: #298; partner-tag-action; partner-tag-action-moves

When the leader lets go of the charged pair on the ground, sub_800CCB0 puts Tails in pose 0xDE, and sub_800E880 stands it up when the pose has played twice, which is what partner-tag-action-moves shows on plain ground, on all 830 frames. Standing on Route 99's breakable wall the original has Tails up in the fourth frame instead, in partner-tag-action, and what ends the wait there wasn't found, so a count of frames stands in for it on an object. Three other things differ, and no recording goes that far: a throw that starts on that wall after walking along it (Tails's sprite 1 pixel off, on the frame of the release), a throw on plain ground just left of it (4 frames after the release), and Tails's own follow-the-leader choices 68 frames after a throw, which are the partner's actions 10 to 18 and its return when it falls far behind, neither made yet. A long charged run down a slope on Route 99 act 1 also has a character 1 pixel off, after 940 matching frames.
