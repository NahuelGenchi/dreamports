# All of X Zone can be played by written presses, as the three bosses hold still enough to time.

About: X Zone; x-zone-the-moon-opens

Proof: #93; x-zone-the-moon-opens

The Egg Wrecker's ship is too high for a jump from the floor, and only the ledge on the right, around x 931 to 979, reaches it. Standing on the floor at x 984 the ball never touches Sonic, and a hit with the ball on the left, while the ship is near x 840 to 870, leaves him free to land. Each swing takes 256 frames, so a hit comes every 512 frames with him back on the floor between. Past it, a running jump from the falling bricks near x 1190 lands on the upper crates, and a second one near x 1285 clears the wall. The Drillster falls to chasing it, and the spring at x 1900 needs landing on from above. Egg X falls to chasing it too, at a cost of its rings. The recording's presses do all this with four hits on each of the first two and eight on Egg X, on one life.
