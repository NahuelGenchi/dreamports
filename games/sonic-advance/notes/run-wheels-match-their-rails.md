# The run wheels' short moves match the rails drawn in Angel Island, so they aren't a bug to fix.

About: Angel Island Zone act 1

Proof: #88

A wheel moves 2 px every 12.5 px of travel, and a diagonal one 1 px each way, as its direction is rounded down to whole pixels. With the fraction kept, five of act 1's wheels ran with right held went 25 to 100% further, past the end of the rail drawn behind them, and the rail no longer reached the wheel. Unchanged, each of those wheels stopped at its rail's end.
