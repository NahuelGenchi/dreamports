# The character select's song differs in sound about 310 frames in, wherever it plays, so where a press falls can show it or not.

About: Time Attack; The character select; time-attack-menu; character-select

Proof: #550

The Time Attack menu plays the character select's song. With the menu opened by A at frame 1730 and left alone, frames 2041 and 2048 to 2049 differ by their sound alone, about 310 frames after the press, around 125 Hz and 250 Hz with the original the louder. The character-select recording first differs by sound at frame 1961, about as long after its press. In the menu, B pressed at frames 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022 or 2025 gave no frame that differs. B at 2017 gave frame 2034, at 2020 frames 2034 to 2037, at 2023 frame 2040, and at 2028 frames 2041 to 2045, all by sound alone. Why the song differs there isn't known.
