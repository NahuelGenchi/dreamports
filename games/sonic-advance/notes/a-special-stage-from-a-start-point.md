# A special stage lines up from a start point, the original dropped on the spring and the address at the stage.

About: amy-special-stage; tails-special-stage; knuckles-special-stage

Proof: #90; amy-special-stage

With `special=1` and `character` in the address the Dreamport opens at the stage. In the original a stretch of `onlyInOriginal` goes through the menus and the character select to the act, and 300 frames in writes the player's place, at 0x03005A20 and 0x03005A24 in 1/256 px, and the camera's, at 0x030059D0 and 0x030059D2, so Amy falls 90 px onto the spring at (10926, 488). Writing the camera matters, as the spring isn't made until the camera comes near it, and a player already inside it is pushed out to its side. As Amy the stretch is 2,224 frames, and each Right fewer in the character select takes 20 off.
