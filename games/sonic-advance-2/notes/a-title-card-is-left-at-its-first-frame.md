# Pressing A on a stage's first frame leaves its title card with nothing of it shown.

About: The stage intro and results; leaf-forest-opening

Proof: #496; leaf-forest-opening

Leaf Forest act 1's task list starts at frame 2316 of the presses leaf-forest-opening uses, and A at that frame ends the title card before any of it draws. The countdown then takes 180 frames instead of 310, the numbers show a frame later, and the stage's frame count, which blinks the ring number, starts from there. So the card can be made later, as its own part, with what comes after it already proven.
