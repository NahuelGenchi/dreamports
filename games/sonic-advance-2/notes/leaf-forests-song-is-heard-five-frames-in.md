# Leaf Forest's song lines up with the original when it starts five frames into the stage.

About: leaf-forest-opening

Proof: #496; leaf-forest-opening

The decompilation starts the song as the stage is made, but with that start, or one to four frames later, leaf-forest-opening's sound differs around frames 135 and 154, and with six later around frames 28 and 168. Five frames later every frame matches. The cause wasn't found, so the number is measured and not explained. Frames 0 and 1 of the recording aren't compared, as they hold what's left of the character select's sound.
