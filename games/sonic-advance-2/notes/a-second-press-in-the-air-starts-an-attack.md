# A second press of the jump button in the air starts an attack that isn't made.

About: A character jumping; leaf-forest-jumping

Proof: #498; leaf-forest-jumping

A press while the first jump is still in the air made 15 to 17 frames differ in tries with it, with a sprite and a sound only the original has. By its numbers a full jump with the button held lasts about 59 frames, so the recordings press again only once he has landed. The decompilation's Player_TryMidAirAction has what that press does, and the homing attack it can turn into needs enemies.
