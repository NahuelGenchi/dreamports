# Three of Leaf Forest act 1's springs can be played with no object that isn't made coming on the screen first.

About: Springs; leaf-forest-spring; leaf-forest-spring-twice; leaf-forest-spring-running; leaf-forest-spring-diagonal

Proof: #500; leaf-forest-spring; leaf-forest-spring-twice; leaf-forest-spring-running; leaf-forest-spring-diagonal

Sonic stands on the ground from frame 0 at 4325, 977 and at 4360, 977, left of an upward spring at the platform's right edge, and at 8210, 1850, left of a diagonal one. Set off by the countdown's end with nothing pressed, from 4325 he reaches the spring under 2.5 pixels a frame, so it takes the usual animation. From 4360 with right held, and from 8210, he's over that, so it takes the fast one. Past the upward spring's right side the ground is about 160 pixels lower, and an object comes on the screen at frame 325 with him falling there. On the diagonal spring's far side, with right held from frame 306, one comes on the screen at frame 364. The ground under a start point has to be found by trying: at 4300, 977 he falls to 1185, and from anywhere under 8155, 1850 a wall stops him as he sets off. Past frame 370 the song's own difference starts, whichever spring is played.
