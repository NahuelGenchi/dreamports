# Three of Leaf Forest act 1's springs can be played with no object that isn't made coming on the screen first.

About: Springs; leaf-forest-spring; leaf-forest-spring-running; leaf-forest-spring-diagonal

Proof: #500; leaf-forest-spring; leaf-forest-spring-running; leaf-forest-spring-diagonal

From a start point at 4496, 930, Sonic drops on an upward spring, and every frame matches up to at least frame 363, when something comes on the screen at its top left corner. From 4360, 950, running right from frame 177 reaches the same spring fast enough for the fast animation, and an object comes on the screen at frame 325. From 8368, 1820, he drops on a diagonal spring, and every frame matches to 203 with nothing pressed. The small grey machine beside Sonic at the start of a stage, or wherever a start point puts him, is the countdown's, not a spring. No recording reaches a sideways or downward spring yet.
