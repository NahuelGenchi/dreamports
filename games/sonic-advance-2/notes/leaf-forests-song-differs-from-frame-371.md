# Leaf Forest's song differs from the original in places from frame 371, whatever the player does.

About: leaf-forest-opening; leaf-forest-running

Proof: #497; leaf-forest-running

With leaf-forest-running's start point and Sonic standing still for 1,800 frames, every picture matches, and the sound differs in 22 stretches between frames 371 and 927, and in none after that. The first is frames 371 to 375, by about 10 dB around 2,000 Hz on both sides. Measured over the whole recording, the two aren't a few frames apart: the song is in time, and single sounds in it are there in one and not in the other, some low and some high. Pressing buttons at other times doesn't move them, and neither does leaving out the announcer's Go, nor starting the character select's song and the announcer's Sonic before the stage, nor five to eight DirectSound channels. Starting the song earlier than five frames in makes this stretch shorter and adds others. The cause wasn't found. It wasn't looked at in the original's sound engine as it runs, as the tools don't show that.
