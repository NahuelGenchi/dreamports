# The number of rings changes how quickly a character gets going, and how soon its boost starts.

About: Rings; leaf-forest-rings-12; leaf-forest-rings-150

Proof: #499; leaf-forest-rings-12; leaf-forest-rings-150

0x0802989C sets a threshold from the ring count, at more than 10, then 50, 100 and 150, and the character's acceleration comes from it. Held right from frame 177, with 12 rings every frame matches to 376, when an object comes on the screen, and with 150 every frame matches to 265, when the boost's particles come on the screen. The boost isn't made.
