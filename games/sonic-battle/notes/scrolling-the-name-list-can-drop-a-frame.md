# How a scroll of the name list shows depends on the song, and the Dreamport only has the commonest way.

About: The name entry, at the first start; first-start; 0x08036350; 0x080368dc; 0x08036428; 0x08018654

Proof: #465; first-start

A scroll redraws the grid, 0x08036350, then the cursor's square redraws its letter, 0x08036428, in the same frame, and 0x08018654 runs the sound engine from the display's second line, in the middle of that work. A test pressed a scroll every 16 frames from frame 890, 70 times down from the grid's last row, twice round the list, then 35 times up from its second row. Going up, the old letter showed for that frame in all 35. Going down, the display is at that square by then: all its lines showed the old letter in 36, a mix of old, nothing and new in 26, and the new letter in 8. The original also lost a frame on 29 of the 105, which leaves the background's scroll a frame behind until the screen ends. The same place in the list differed between its two rounds, so the song matters: with 4 voices playing or fewer it lost a frame on 2 of 64 scrolls, with 5 on 14 of 27, and with 6 or more on 13 of 14. A straight-line fit of the letters' pixels and the sound's work was right on about 85 in every 100 scrolls it wasn't fitted to. So the Dreamport always shows the old letter for that frame and never loses one, and `first-start` scrolls only where the original does the same, chosen by trying moments: six of the seven tried for its holding of Up differed. A scroll down at another moment can differ by a few pixels of one letter for one frame, or by the lost frame.
