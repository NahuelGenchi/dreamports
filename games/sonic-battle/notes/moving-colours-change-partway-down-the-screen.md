# The arena's moving colours land where the frame's work and the sound's mixing take them, partway down the screen.

About: A fight's opening; fight-opening; 0x08011c7c

Proof: #447; #545; fight-opening

The second half of 0x08011c7c writes the colours as it runs, after a V-blank. Its work, about 82,000 cycles as mGBA counts its instructions, usually ends just before the interrupt at line 1 (0x080185cc, with DISPSTAT's 0x128). When it doesn't, SoundMain takes the console's cycles first, 20,000 to 55,000 by how many channels play and how far their samples move, and the lines drawn by then keep the old colours: rows up to 33, 31, 30, 31 and 29 at frames 56, 104, 120, 136 and 160 of `fight-opening`, and at 144 row 31 has pixels of both palettes. Counting those cycles gives all 400 of its frames. Only frames 48 to 160's landings from row 25 down show, and those take `LINE_ONE_CONSTANT` anywhere from 20 to 160, so 90 sits in the middle. The sound's own count (#545) left no room for it until the start point counted the sound frames it skips: the engine's envelopes step every 15 frames and its DMA restarts every 9, so without them CgbSound's cost fell on the wrong frames, by about 250 cycles; with them it's right to 6 cycles on every frame from 40 to 397. Measured in mGBA after that, on most frames the kit's count is 330 to 360 cycles over SoundMain from 0x0804834c to its return, and on some it's up to 200 more than that, or further off on a few frames with no landing that shows, which nobody has explained. The longer choice screen of 2,020 frames that differed by a line or two in 10 of its 250 frames with changing colours was never kept, and hasn't been run again.
