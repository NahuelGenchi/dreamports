# Thirty-nine kinds of thing fall and meet walls, not only the four Sonic's specials use.

About: Special attacks; specials-power; specials-shot; specials-set; shadow-specials-power; shadow-specials-shot; shadow-specials-set; 0x0800CEC8; 0x0800D260

Proof: #460; shadow-specials-shot; shadow-specials-set

0x0800CEC8's switch sends thirty-nine kinds of thing to 0x0800D260, which pushes a thing out of a wall or an arena edge and drops it to the floor. The four kinds Sonic's specials use are among them, as are Shadow's shot, set and their blasts, 157, 158, 160 and 161 to 164. Without those steps a thing keeps the height it started at, and its sprite stands a pixel high once the camera moves; the first frames of shadow-specials-shot and shadow-specials-set show it.
