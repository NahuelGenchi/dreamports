# The intro is frames 390 to 546: five pictures over 65 frames, then a 91-frame animation that ends on two black frames.

About: The intro; logos-and-intro

Proof: #191; logos-and-intro

The intro (0x0803d748) starts where the logos' black screen leaves off, at frame 390. Each of its five pictures (0x0803d860) takes 14 frames as it moves toward its target, and the fifth takes 8 before the animation begins. The animation (0x0803dd14) runs 91 frames: its scale moves from 1 to 256, out to 320 at its frame 60 and back to 176 at its frame 75, and it clears the palette on its last frame. Frames 545 and 546 are black and go with the intro, as the rule about one-colour frames says; the title display at 0x0803e1b0 starts after them. The recording logos-and-intro, 547 frames from power-on with nothing pressed, proves the part.
