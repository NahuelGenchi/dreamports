## What it adds

- **The real Kirby.** His moves, his 24 copy abilities, his sprites and his sounds all come from your own copy of the DS game.
- **Inhale, swallow, copy.** Hold attack to suck an enemy in, then press down to swallow it and copy a random power. Or spit it back out as a star.
- **Weapons turn into powers.** Kirby can’t hold a sword, so every weapon and skill you find becomes an ability item: inhale it and swallow it to copy that power.
- **The real Dead Cells.** The same levels, enemies, bosses, shops and unlocks, with a few rules changed to fit Kirby.

## Play in four steps

1. Tick the box in [Before you play](#play) and download the Linux `.AppImage`.
2. Right-click it, open *Properties* and turn on *Allow running as a program*. Then double-click it.
3. The launcher looks for Steam, Dead Cells and your Kirby game by itself. Press *Set up Kirby* once, and it builds your Kirby from your own game files in about two minutes.
4. Press *Play*, and choose *New game* to start a Kirby run.

On Windows, the test version is a `.zip`: right-click it, choose *Extract all*, open the folder and double-click *Kirby’s Dead Land*.
