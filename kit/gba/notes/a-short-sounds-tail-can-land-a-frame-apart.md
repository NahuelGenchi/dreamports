# A short sound's last step can land a frame apart by when it was pressed, so prove can mark one frame by its sound.

About: the sound hardware; kit/gba/sound/apu.js; reading a sound that differs; name-entry

Proof: #300

On Sonic Advance 3's name entry, three versions of a recording with more than forty B presses each gave no frame that differs, one, and one. That frame came 13 frames after a press, by its sound alone: around 2,000 Hz on the left, the original at 0.3 or 0.4 of a step and the Dreamport at 3.3, 10.3 or 10.4 dB apart against the 10 dB `prove` allows. Moving the presses before it a frame later took that frame away and showed the same on another B press, so it goes by when the press falls and not by what the game does. What makes it depend on the timing wasn't measured. The recording that's kept, name-entry, presses B 20 times and has no frame that differs.
