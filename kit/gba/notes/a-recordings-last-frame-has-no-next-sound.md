# A recording's last frame can differ by its sound alone, as there's no frame after it to measure with.

About: recordings; the sound `prove` compares; a part's last frame

Proof: #394

In Crash Bandicoot Purple Dreamport's `logos`, a recording of 519 frames ended on the last frame of its part, and `prove` found that frame's sound 10.1 dB apart in one band, just over the 10 it allows. The same frames in a recording of 525 frames came out the same, with nothing else changed. A frame's sound is measured with the frame before it and the one after it, so the last one of a recording is measured over less.
